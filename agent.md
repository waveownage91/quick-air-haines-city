# 🚀 Project: Quick Air Haines City (Lead Gen Site)

## 🎯 Objective
Generate inbound AC repair leads in Haines City, FL and monetize them by selling to local HVAC contractors.

Primary KPI:
- Number of inbound calls (tracked)
- Number of form submissions
- Revenue per lead / monthly contract value

---

## 🧠 Strategy Overview

This is NOT a traditional SaaS or content site.

This is a:
> Lead Generation Funnel disguised as a service website

Flow:
1. User searches or clicks ad
2. Lands on site
3. Calls or submits form
4. Lead is forwarded to contractor
5. Contractor pays per lead or monthly

---

## 🏗️ Tech Stack

- Frontend: Streamlit
- Hosting: Streamlit Cloud / VPS
- Domain: quickairhainescity.com
- Call Tracking: CallRail or Twilio
- Automation: Zapier (optional)

## 📞 Call Handling Logic

- All calls routed via tracking number
- Calls forwarded to contractor
- Calls logged for reporting and billing

IMPORTANT:
Agent should NEVER answer calls manually.

---

## 💰 Monetization Model

Phase 1:
- Free leads to contractor (validation)

Phase 2:
- Pay per lead ($30–$60)

Phase 3:
- Monthly retainer ($500–$1,000+)

---

## 📈 Traffic Strategy

### Phase 1 (Immediate)
- Google Ads
- High intent keywords:
  - “AC repair Haines City”
  - “emergency AC repair near me”

### Phase 2 (Mid-term)
- Local SEO
- Google Business Profile
- Citations (Yelp, directories)

### Phase 3 (Scale)
- Add more cities
- Expand services (plumbing, roofing)
